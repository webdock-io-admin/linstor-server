Another instance of the equal-UUID reconciliation, on DRBD 9.3.4 (GIT-hash e59b287b199f7224468af85b68a10e83c68213f5, drbd-utils 9.35.0, kernel 7.2.11-zabbly+, configuration generated by LINSTOR 1.35.2), with a different consequence: an UpToDate diskful node became SyncTarget for its **whole device**.

Resource R3, vol0 10 GiB: diskful A and B, diskless Primary D (writing), plus diskless members. 2026-10-28, times CET. B's replica was being removed (LINSTOR `resource delete`; B's `down` was declined by the Primary, "Aborting cluster-wide state change rv=-10"). What stands out:

- A's own bitmap toward B held **2533701 bits, the whole device**, before the handshake, although A and B had been `Established` until 13:02:04.846 and B was gone for only 0.6 s. Its bitmap UUID was 0, and the current UUIDs were equal (`C0925277B1393298`).
- `no-sync by rule=lost-quorum` was then overridden by the reconciliation, which made A, UpToDate and connected to the writing Primary, the **SyncTarget**. A went `UpToDate → Outdated → Inconsistent` for a full 10 GiB resync. It was cut off one second later when B's removal continued. A later full resync of A took 15 s and moved 53.5 MB of real data.
- vol1 of the same resource had 77655 bits on A toward B; there the bitmap was simply cleared ("cleared bm UUID and bitmap") with no resync.
- Quorum was held by A and the diskless members throughout ("Would lose quorum, but using tiebreaker logic to keep").

The whole-device bitmap may be the drbdmeta case described in the previous comment (attach without the bitmap provenance feature, so all out-of-sync bits count as set); our excerpt starts at the disconnect and does not show A's last attach, so we cannot confirm it from these lines.

As in this issue, the reconciliation picks the side to sync from stale bits and ignores which side is current. Here it cost redundancy (A Inconsistent next to a node being deleted) rather than the Primary's I/O. Where would a whole-device bitmap toward a just-disconnected peer come from? And should a node that is UpToDate and connected to the Primary ever be chosen as SyncTarget by this rule?
